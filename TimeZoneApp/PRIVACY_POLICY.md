# Privacy Policy for World Clocks

**Last Updated: [Date]**

## Introduction

Welcome to World Clocks! This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

**World Clocks does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

Our app is designed with your privacy in mind:

- **No User Accounts**: You don't need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.
- **No Tracking**: We do not track your usage, location, or any other data.
- **No Analytics**: We do not use any analytics services that collect data about your usage.
- **No Advertising**: The app contains no ads, so no ad networks are collecting data.

## Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following information **only on your device**:

- **Selected Timezones**: The timezones you choose to display are stored locally using iOS's UserDefaults and App Groups for widget functionality.
- **Preferences**: Any app settings or preferences you configure.

This data **never leaves your device** and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## Widget Functionality

To provide widget functionality, the app uses iOS's App Groups feature to share your selected timezones between the main app and the widget extension. This data sharing:

- Occurs **only on your device**
- Is **not transmitted** to any servers
- Is **not accessible** to any third parties
- Is **only used** to display your selected timezones in the widget

## Third-Party Services

World Clocks does not use any third-party services, libraries, or SDKs that collect, process, or transmit user data.

## Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your personal information through our app.

## Children's Privacy

World Clocks does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we may have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
