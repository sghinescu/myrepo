# Lesson 2 — Conversational Gravity & Emotional Load-Bearing

[Lesson 1](lesson1-foundations.md) · **Lesson 2** · [Lesson 3](lesson3-taxonomy-of-gaps.md) · [Lesson 4](lesson4-conversational-vector.md)

> The earlier curriculum outline names four failure modes and COS operators. The labels and examples below are working formulations.

## Concepts

- **Conversational gravity** is the steadiness that lets another person speak at their own pace. It combines **non-reactivity** (pause before responding), **spaciousness** (leave time and room), and **low ego** (stay with their experience).
- **Emotional load-bearing** is staying present while someone shares a difficult feeling and retaining enough composure to listen and respond. You can acknowledge the feeling and let the speaker decide what support they want.

## Mechanism and COS integration

Use **See → Signal → Invite** as a non-invasion sequence.

1. **See** the words or change in delivery. Pause and notice your impulse to act.
2. **Signal** that you heard them with a brief reflection or acknowledgment.
3. **Invite** one further step if they stay engaged. Accept a decline, a correction, or a change of topic.

Working COS operators: **Pause → Ground → Reflect → Offer choice**. Pausing creates time to choose a response. Reflection lets the speaker correct your read. Choice preserves their control. Combine with Lesson 1’s +1 rule when the speaker adds depth.

| Failure mode | What it sounds like | Load-bearing move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixing | “Here’s what you should do.” | “Would listening or thinking it through help more right now?” |
| Minimizing | “It could be worse.” | “That sounds like it’s been weighing on you.” |
| Taking over | “That happened to me too. Let me tell you…” | “What part has been hardest for you?” |
| Probing | “Tell me exactly what they said.” | “We can stay here or leave it there.” |

## Example and practice

> “I got the result yesterday. I don’t really want to talk about it.”
>
> “Okay. I’m here if you want company.”

Recognize the explicit boundary, then practice responding in one sentence. If they later say “I’m scared about what happens next,” you can reflect the uncertainty and wait for their next move.

## Handwritten notes

- Gravity = non-reactivity + spaciousness + low ego.
- Hold difficult feeling with presence and choice.
- See → Signal → Invite. Respect a boundary on the first pass.
- Watch for fixing, minimizing, taking over, and probing.

## Field card

When someone shares a mild frustration, notice your first impulse. Wait a beat and reflect one phrase. Offer a next step only if they stay engaged. Record how they responded.
