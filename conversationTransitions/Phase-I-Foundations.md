# Phase I — Mechanics (Foundational)

## Objective
Build the core mechanics of conversational transitions before learning advanced calibration.

### Module 1: Spotting Doors
A conversational door is any moment where deeper exploration becomes possible.

Primary door categories:
- Emotion
- Evaluation
- Change

Conversation formula:
Statement → Door Detection → Transition

### Module 2: Choosing the Right Door
Hierarchy:
Facts → Evaluation → Emotion → Meaning → Identity

Golden Rule:
Go one layer deeper than the speaker.

When multiple doors appear, ask:
"Which door explains the others?"

Follow the root door rather than the branch.

### Module 3: The Transition Ladder
Pressure and depth are separate dimensions.

Ladder:
Echo → Observation → Curiosity → Experience → Meaning → Identity

Rules:
- One rung at a time.
- Match, then lead.
- Expansion earns expansion.
- Contraction earns softness.
- Stay around 80% of the maximum possible depth.

Core mindset:
Depth is earned by disclosure, not by time.
Questions are earned, not imposed.
